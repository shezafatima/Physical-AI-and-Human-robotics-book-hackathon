# ADR 001: Coursebook Content Depth

## Status
Accepted

## Context
The coursebook needs to provide comprehensive coverage of Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics topics to serve as an effective educational resource. There was a decision to be made about the depth of content for each chapter and whether to focus on breadth or depth.

## Decision
We will adopt a comprehensive approach that provides detailed explanations, examples, and interactive elements for each topic. Each chapter will include:

- Overview and learning objectives
- Detailed theory with explanations
- Interactive diagrams and visualizations
- Code samples with explanations
- Exercises and quizzes
- References and further reading

## Rationale
This approach ensures that students receive thorough coverage of each topic, which is essential for understanding complex subjects like Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics. The comprehensive approach also allows for different learning styles through various interactive elements.

## Consequences
- Positive: Students will have thorough understanding of each topic
- Positive: Rich, engaging learning experience
- Negative: More development time required
- Negative: Larger content size
