# ADR 003: Chapter Count

## Status
Accepted

## Context
The coursebook needs to have an appropriate number of chapters to cover the Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics curriculum effectively. There was a decision to be made about the minimum number of chapters required.

## Decision
We will implement a minimum of 8 chapters, each focusing on a specific aspect of Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics:

1. Introduction to Physical AI
2. ROS 2 Fundamentals
3. Robot Simulation with Gazebo & Unity
4. NVIDIA Isaac Platform
5. Humanoid Robot Development
6. Vision-Language-Action (VLA)
7. Conversational Robotics & GPT Integration
8. Capstone Project: Autonomous Humanoid

## Rationale
Eight chapters provide sufficient depth to cover the core topics while maintaining a manageable structure. This allows for comprehensive coverage of Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics without overwhelming students. Each chapter can be completed in a reasonable timeframe.

## Consequences
- Positive: Comprehensive coverage of core topics
- Positive: Manageable chapter length
- Positive: Clear progression through topics
- Negative: May require additional chapters for comprehensive coverage
