# ADR 006: Citation Style

## Status
Accepted

## Context
The coursebook needs to follow a consistent citation style for references to maintain academic standards. There was a decision to be made about which citation format to use.

## Decision
We will use APA (American Psychological Association) citation style throughout the coursebook for all references, including:

- In-text citations with author-date format
- Reference lists at the end of chapters
- Proper formatting for different types of sources (books, articles, websites, etc.)
- Consistent citation format across all chapters

## Rationale
APA style is widely recognized in academic settings and provides clear, consistent formatting for citations. It's particularly appropriate for technical and scientific content like Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics.

## Consequences
- Positive: Academic credibility and consistency
- Positive: Clear attribution of sources
- Positive: Standardized format familiar to many students
- Negative: Requires careful attention to citation formatting
- Negative: May need to convert citations from other formats
